# Privacy Policy for Shambala Music

**Last Updated:** October 6, 2026

**Shambala Music** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **Shambala Music** mobile application (Package Name: `com.Shambala_Music`). This Privacy Policy informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information when using our mobile application and divine music/meditation streaming services.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our App, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that we request will be retained by us and used as described in this privacy policy.

### Types of Data Collected:
* **Account & Authentication Data:** If you create an account or log in via Firebase Authentication, we may collect your email address, display name, and authentication tokens to sync your playlist preferences, favorites, and guest/member status across devices.
* **Usage & Analytics Data:** We collect anonymous usage data such as app activity, song playback counts, 12:00 PM noon meditation participation metrics, and crash logs to improve app performance.
* **Device Information:** We may collect device model, OS version, unique device identifiers, and network connection type to ensure seamless audio streaming and offline playback caching.

---

## 2. Permissions Used by the App

To provide full functionality, **Shambala Music** requests the following Android permissions:

* **INTERNET (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Required to stream online audio tracks, divine bhajans, podcasts, and 24/7 live stream feeds.
* **LOCAL STORAGE / CACHING:** Used locally on your device to store temporary audio cache files for offline listening.
* **NOTIFICATIONS (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used solely to deliver optional reminders for the daily 12:00 PM Noon Meditation session and background audio playback controls.
* **FOREGROUND SERVICE (`android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`):** Allows continuous audio playback when the app is minimized or the device screen is off.
* **WAKE LOCK (`android.permission.WAKE_LOCK`):** Prevents device processor from sleeping during active meditation sessions.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our services are suitable for a general audience of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.
